# Grand Theft Auto VI The Biggest Launch of the Decade

Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar Games' next open-world crime epic, and one of the most anticipated games ever made. After more than a decade since GTA V, the series returns to Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.

---

## Background

> "The biggest entertainment launch of the decade."

GTA VI follows two protagonists, **Jason** and **Lucia**, in a modern take on Vice City.

- Developer: **Rockstar Games**
- Publisher: **Take-Two Interactive**
- Platforms at launch: **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**
- Release date: **November 19, 2026**

## The Good and the Bad

** The Good**
- A huge, modern open-world setting in Vice City and Leonida
- Two protagonists promise a story built around a duo
- Rockstar's track record for world detail, writing, and polish
- Visuals that look like a true next-gen showcase

** The Bad**
- Delayed twice already, which adds a lot of pressure to deliver
- Console-only at launch, with PC likely a year or more away
- Hype is so high that almost anything short of perfect could feel like a letdown

---

## Conclusion

GTA VI is my pick for the Game of the Year. It has the scale, the budget, and the hype to redefine open-world gaming, as long as it lives up to the wait.

> "Some games are released. GTA VI is an event."

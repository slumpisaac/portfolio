# Resident Evil Requiem Horror Royalty Returns

Resident Evil Requiem is **Capcom's** newest entry in the survival horror series, which has been running for 30 years. It mixes slow, tense horror with big action to please both kinds of fans.

---

## Background

> "Horror royalty returns."

The story follows **Grace Ashcroft** in tense survival horror, while longtime hero **Leon S. Kennedy** brings the action side.

- Developer: **Capcom**
- Platforms: **PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2**
- Release date: **February 27, 2026**
- Ties back to the events of **Raccoon City**

## The Good and the Bad

**The Good**
- Switching between Grace (tense survival horror) and Leon (action) keeps things fresh
- Excellent gunplay, with strong puzzles and exploration
- Top-tier audio, visuals, and voice acting
- Plenty of nostalgic callbacks for longtime fans

**The Bad**
- The story is uneven and can swing from engaging to goofy
- The action-heavy second half is less compelling than the horror
- Grace gets little character development
- Heavy on fan service, which may overwhelm newcomers

---

## Conclusion

Resident Evil Requiem proves the series still has life after 30 years. It is my second-place GOTY pick, and it stays just behind GTA VI on my list.
